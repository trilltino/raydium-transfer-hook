Permissionless program selection. The platform admin may choose any program ID. Do not introduce a Raydium

10.1 Anti-bundle


Rule. Configurable max qualifying buys per slot/block, modeled after Hooked's public anti-bundle rule. Use
Clock.slot and a per-mint counter state.
Trade classification. Do not count every wallet-to-wallet transfer as a buy. Identify qualifying buy transfers through

14. Security and trust boundary








An arbitrary hook can freeze buys, sells, withdrawals, migration, claims, or any other transfer. Treat the program ID
and upgrade authority as material risk information.
Token-2022 strips signer privileges from the original transfer accounts before the hook CPI, but extra accounts can
still be writable. Validate and minimize writable state.
Never assume a hook remains stable because the mint program ID is unchanged. The hook program may be
upgradeable and the ExtraAccountMetaList may change.
If the mint TransferHook authority can change the hook program after launch, define who holds that authority. For
the platform model, prefer a platform PDA/governance or revoke authority when immutability is required.
Hook rejection must never leave partial reward/commitment state. Rely on Solana atomicity in deployed code and
test it on a validator.
